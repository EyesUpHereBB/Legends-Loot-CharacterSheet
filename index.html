Use the examples below to choose the attribute that best matches what your character is attempting.

💪Strength

Raw physical power, force, and athletic effort.

When to use Strength

Breaking, lifting, pushing, climbing, jumping, grappling, or striking with force.

Break a doorLift a boulderGrappleClimb
“I force the rusted gate open.”

🪶Dexterity

Agility, balance, reflexes, stealth, and precision.

When to use Dexterity

Dodging, sneaking, balancing, picking locks, delicate handling, or accurate movement.

Sneak past guardsPick a lockKeep balanceCatch an object
“I quietly move behind the sentry.”

❤️Constitution

Endurance, stamina, health, and physical resilience.

When to use Constitution

Resisting poison, disease, exhaustion, harsh weather, pain, or prolonged physical strain.

Resist poisonEndure coldHold breathMarch longer
“I keep moving despite the toxic fumes.”

📖Intelligence

Reasoning, memory, education, and careful analysis.

When to use Intelligence

Recalling lore, investigating clues, solving puzzles, decoding writing, or understanding complex systems.

Recall loreStudy a clueSolve a puzzleDecode runes
“Do I recognize this ancient symbol?”

👁️Wisdom

Awareness, instinct, perception, and practical judgment.

When to use Wisdom

Noticing danger, reading behavior, tracking, treating injuries, surviving outdoors, or trusting intuition.

Search a roomTrack footprintsRead emotionsForage
“Do I sense that the guide is lying?”

🎭Charisma

Presence, confidence, leadership, and social influence.

When to use Charisma

Persuading, deceiving, intimidating, performing, negotiating, or commanding attention.

NegotiateIntimidateBluffPerform
“I convince the guard to let us pass.”
